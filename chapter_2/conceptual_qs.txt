1. For each of parts (a) through (d), indicate whether we would generally expect the performance of a flexible statistical learning method to be better or worse than an inflexible method. Justify your answer.
(a) The sample size n is extremely large, and the number of predictors p is small.
(b) The number of predictors p is extremely large, and the number of observations n is small.
(c) The relationship between the predictors and response is highly non-linear.
(d) The variance of the error terms, i.e. σ2 = Var(ε), is extremely high.

a)
Answer: a more flexible method is preferred
Justification:
- Flexible methods don't have information on what the model's shape will look like so they demand more info. 
- if the number of predictors is large, there are more shapes of f(x) that are available
- A.K.A if there are more dimensions, the there are more curves which fit the data so to find the true shape of the curve,
- more data is needed


b) unflexible method is preferred for the reasons above

c) flexible is better. 
Justification:
- Reduces bias and since we don't know how non-linear it is, we can't set the parameters to an exact degree

d) less flexible model:

Justification:
- Flexible models read into noise more than unflexible models
- High variance in training data = more noise in training data
- to mitigate the consequences of noise, less flexible model should be chosen
- flexible modles have to read into the noise because more functions are possible, and they have to come up with something that has lowest MSE.
